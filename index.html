Forex Fundamentals

Market Structure & Participants

The Forex market is decentralized (OTC). Participants include central banks, commercial banks, corporations, institutional investors, and retail traders. Understanding behavior helps anticipate liquidity and price movements.

Currency Pairs & Pips

Currency pairs are expressed as BASE/QUOTE (e.g., EUR/USD). Pips represent the smallest price increments. Traders calculate pip value to manage risk and profits.

Trading Sessions & Volatility

Forex operates 24/5 across Tokyo, London, and New York. Volatility peaks during overlapping sessions, offering tighter spreads and better trading opportunities.

Lot Sizes & Leverage

Standard lot = 100,000 units, mini = 10,000, micro = 1,000. Leverage allows controlling large positions with small capital. Use margin responsibly.

Risk Management

Position Sizing

Position size = Risk Amount ÷ (Stop-loss pips × Pip Value per lot). Risk 1–2% per trade to protect capital.

Stop-loss & Take Profit

Stop-loss orders limit potential losses; take-profit orders lock in gains. Always calculate position size based on risk tolerance before placing trades.
